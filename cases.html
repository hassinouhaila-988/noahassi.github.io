From change requests to a sequenced roadmap

Grouped change requests (Google Pay, card tokenisation, pick-up points, scent profile, gift reminders and more) into 6 epics, mapped dependencies across systems PSP, PIM, CRM, OMS, WMS and the AI engine, and sequenced iterations so no two features compete for the same team or checkout step. Then wrote use cases, open points, user stories and acceptance criteria for two features.
