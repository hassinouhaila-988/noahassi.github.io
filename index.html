About
me!

I started as a Business Analyst, sitting between stakeholders and developers and learning both languages in the most structured industry: automotive.
Today I own the backlog for a retail Promotion Management System used across Europe, the USA and Hong Kong. What hasn't changed is my obsession with the product design, the definition of clear requirements to cover the corner cases  as well as defining a clear path to deliver the most usable and aligned to business needs product.

Business constraints

What the business can afford

Aligning 15+ stakeholders, discuss priorities and deadlines across the  concurrent retail projects and multiple countries. Define and refine the business requirements and milestones to accomplish.

At the centre

What the user really needs

User requirements elicitation defining clear and compliant user stories with linked  acceptance criteria. Conduct UX reviews in Figma as well as handoff to devs to align requirement vision.

this is where I live
TECHNICAL DEPENDENCIES

What the system needs to do?

Definition of dependencies  between the involved system and others in the same ecosystem. Extracting system specs, conducting end-to-end test cases to validate development. 

beyond the backlog

Organizing with a Method

Capacity

Capacity planning

Sizing each sprint against the team's real availability, so commitments are realistic and releases land on time.

Allocation

Task allocation

Assigning stories by skill set and dependency, balancing work across developers and number concurrent retail projects based on the project priority and timeline. 

Delivery

Delivery cadence

Running Planning, Review and Retrospective across 10+ sprints, coordinating 15+ stakeholders on scope and releases.
